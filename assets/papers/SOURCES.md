# Publication figures

Figures are reproduced on the academic homepage of co-author Pangkit Fong. The original research images are preserved; each displayed figure links to its source.

- `finssim.png`: FinsSim, Figure 1, https://arxiv.org/html/2609.23943v1#S2.F1. Original image: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.23943v1/figures/finssim_architecture_5.png. Copyright remains with the paper's authors.
- `tracking.png`: Underwater Visual Target Tracking, Figure 1, https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20731v1#S1.F1. Original image: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20731v1/figures/Fishtrack1.png. Copyright remains with the paper's authors.
- `aucamp.png`: Aucamp platform overview, https://arxiv.org/html/2506.09876v1#S0.F1. Original image: https://arxiv.org/html/2506.09876v1/fig1.png. Copyright remains with the paper's authors.
- `hicrisp.png`: HiCRISP framework, https://ming-bot.github.io/HiCRISP.github.io/. Original image: https://ming-bot.github.io/HiCRISP.github.io/static/images/FRAMEWORK.png. The project page is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); attribution: Chenlin Ming, Jiacheng Lin, Pangkit Fong, Han Wang, Xiaoming Duan, and Jianping He. This image is shared under the same license, without modifications.

- `firmware-framework.png`: FineMote, Figure 2(b), https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04600v1#S2.F2. Original image: https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04600v1/r2.png. Attribution: Wang Xi, Feiran Wei, Mo Deng, Weiheng Lin, Pangkit Fong, Jianping He. Distributed without modification under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), as stated by the paper.
- `phononic-harvesting.svg`: original conceptual schematic prepared for this homepage, based on the paper description and the public publisher summary at https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0020740322001734 (DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmecsci.2022.107253). It illustrates a defect in a phononic plate and piezoelectric energy harvesting. It is not a reproduced paper figure and contains no experimental or simulated results.
- `coverage-attack.svg`: original conceptual schematic prepared for this homepage, based on the owner's research description and https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-19-3998-3_164. It illustrates a node-connected coverage environment and an improved-BFS attack search. The routes are illustrative, not a numerical result or a claim of optimality for the pictured environment.
