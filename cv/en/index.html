About

I am a PhD student in Control Science and Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University's School of Automation and Intelligent Sensing, advised by Prof. Jianping He. I earned my master's degree in Control Engineering at SJTU and bachelor's degree in Aircraft Design at Xi'an Jiaotong University. My research focuses on learning–control fusion for underwater robotics, multi-agent coordination, and robot learning and planning, connecting algorithms, embedded development, and physical robot experiments. I chair the Student Organizing Committee for the 2026 DIVE Robotics Forum, serve as a teaching assistant for Modern Control Theory, and review for IEEE TVT, ICRA, and IROS. I help organize summer schools and academic exchanges, mentor undergraduates, and have participated in IROS 2025 in Hangzhou, PANNS at NUS, and L4DC 2024 in Oxford. In 2026, I led our team to the PIONEER class championship at the MATE ROV Competition's China regional qualifier. I was also an Outstanding Trainee in Tencent's 2025 Rhino-Bird Open-Source Talent Program, Hunyuan LLM track. Other awards include fifth place in the China International University Underwater Robotics Competition's AUV track, a Shanghai Internet+ Silver Award, and a National College Students' Mechanical Design Competition third prize. I also create robotics videos for IWIN-FINS Lab on Bilibili.
