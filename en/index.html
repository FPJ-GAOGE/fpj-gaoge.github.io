Perception · Planning · Control

Perception · Planning · Control

From algorithms to real robotic systems.

I am a PhD student in Control Science and Engineering at the School of Automation and Intelligent Sensing, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Jianping He. I received my master's degree in Control Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and my bachelor's degree in Aircraft Design from Xi'an Jiaotong University. My research focuses on learning–control fusion for underwater robotics, multi-agent coordination, and robot learning and planning. I study how perception, planning, and control work together on real robotic systems. My work also includes mechanical, electronic, and embedded development, connecting algorithm design with experiments on physical robots. I serve as Chair of the Student Organizing Committee for the 2026 DIVE Robotics Forum and as a teaching assistant for the undergraduate course Modern Control Theory. I review for IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, ICRA, IROS, and related venues. I also help organize SJTU Global Summer School projects, international seminars, and visiting-professor exchanges, and mentor undergraduate projects through the Junzheng Program, undergraduate innovation training, and Zhiyuan Future Scholars Program. In 2026, I led our team to the PIONEER class championship at the China regional qualifier of the MATE ROV Competition. In 2025, I was named an Outstanding Trainee in the Hunyuan LLM track of the Tencent Rhino-Bird Open-Source Talent Program. Other awards include 5th place in the AUV track of the China International University Underwater Robotics Competition, a Shanghai Silver Award in the Internet+ Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition, and a third prize in the National College Students' Mechanical Design Competition. I have participated in IROS 2025 in Hangzhou, PANNS at the National University of Singapore, and L4DC 2024 in Oxford, UK. Alongside my research, I create videos for IWIN-FINS Lab on Bilibili and other platforms, sharing research and hands-on work in robotics and topics such as diffusion policies.
